Pending flower time genes

  We selected Arabidopsis thaliana as the model organism and identified its pending flower time genes. By best matching, we identified the pending flower time genes of 25 other Fabaceae. They were identified using pfam and stored in the platform.
   Although flowers generated from different plant species are extensively diversified, the underlying genetic and molecular mechanisms that regulate flower development are highly conserved. We obtained the flowering genes of Arabidopsis from the Flowering Interactive Database, and then determined the number of 1,809 flowering genes of 25 plants in the legume by blast sequence alignment, which was convenient for researchers to browse and download.
  The relevant gene ids can be obtained from the Fabaceae blast and match under the Tools module.This link is Fabaceae blast and match.
