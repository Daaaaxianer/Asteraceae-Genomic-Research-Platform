Aster-Anccalling

Reconstructing ancestral genomes supports the inference of new genomic chromosome karyotypes in Asteraceae and the reconstruction of ancient gene evolutionary histories. We provide detailed information for 29 AAGs, including GFF, PEP, and CDS files for each AAG, along with the number of inferred ancient genes located on ancient chromosomes. These AAGs collectively cover 463 ancient chromosomes and 404,036 ancient genes.
