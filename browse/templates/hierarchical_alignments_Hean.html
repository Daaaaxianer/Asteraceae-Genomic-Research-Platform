Hierarchical alignments with the Helianthus annuus genome as reference

  Inter-genomic and intra-genomic comparisons can help reveal the structural complexity of Asteraceae genomes. We used Helianthus annuus as a reference, and by comparing homologous gene locus maps and Ks values between Helianthus annuus and other Asteraceae, we could separate orthologous and paralogous genes produced by different polyploidization in the species genomes. We constructed a 40×70, 843 macro-matrices.
