Nitrogen fixation genes

  We identified three resistance genes as Nitrogen regulatory protein and Carbon-nitrogen hydrolase. The Nitrogen regulatory protein gene was identified by pfam number PF00543. The Carbon-nitrogen hydrolase gene was identified by pfam number PF00795.
  It is generally found in nature in leguminous plants. It is capable of converting atmospheric nitrogen into the chemosynthetic state fixed in the roots through a series of biochemical reactions. For nitrogen-fixing microorganisms, nitrogen-fixing genes manipulate and regulate the synthesis of nitrogen-fixing enzymes, thus making the nitrogen-fixing microorganisms nitrogen-fixing. If the nitrogen-fixing genes are artificially transferred, it is possible to obtain new species with nitrogen fixation. A lot of research has been conducted in this field and the progress is remarkable. We identified two structural domains related to nitrogen fixation of a total of 262 genes, by screening the pfam results.
