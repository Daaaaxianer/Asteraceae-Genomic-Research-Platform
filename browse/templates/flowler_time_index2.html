Flowler time genes

  We selected Arabidopsis thaliana as the model organism and identified its Flowler time genes. By best matching, we identified the Flowler time genes of 25 other Fabaceae. They were identified using pfam and stored in the platform.
   Flowering in higher plants can be divided into two stages: the transition from stem tip meristem to floral meristem and the formation of floral organs. The former is mainly controlled by flowering genes, while the latter stage is mainly caused by the regulation of plant homologous (Heterotypic) cassette genes. We obtained the flowering genes of Arabidopsis from the Flowering Interactive Database, and our database counted 7,749 flowering genes from 25 legume earlings. flowering genes for easy browsing and downloading by researchers.
