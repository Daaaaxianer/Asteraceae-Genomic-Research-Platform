PCT Event-related genes

  Based on orthologous and paralogous regions identified within and between genomes, we isolated sets of orthologous and paralogous lineages resulting from species divergence and polyploidy events. We determined the evolutionary rates of key evolutionary events in Fabaceae by performing nuclear function analysis of Ks between these orthologous and paralogous relatives.
