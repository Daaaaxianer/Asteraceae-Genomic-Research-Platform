Metabolomics of Plants

  Metabolomics, a subset of systems biology, is crucial for unraveling the complex metabolic pathways of Asteraceae family. Using the NPASS (https://bidd.group/NPASS/index.php) and GNDC (http://www.gndc.org.cn/) databases, metabolome data from 1,500 Asteraceae plants were obtained, and candidate 166,748 genes associated with 16,207 metabolites were screened. This simplifies researchers' exploration of metabolite synthesis genes, facilitating in-depth investigation into the metabolomic characteristics of Asteraceae.
