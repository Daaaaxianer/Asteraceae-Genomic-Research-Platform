Fatty acid genes

  We identified three resistance genes as CRAL-TRIO lipid binding domain, Fatty acid hydroxylase, and Fatty acyl-coenzyme A reductase NAD-binding domain. The CRAL-TRIO lipid binding domain gene was identified by pfam number PF00650. The Fatty acid hydroxylase gene was identified by pfam number PF04116. The Fatty acyl-coenzyme A reductase NAD-binding domain gene was identified by pfam number PF07993.
  Fatty acids (FAs) consist of long hydrophobic, often unbranched chains of hydrocarbons, with hydrophilic carboxylic acid groups at one end. They are an important source of reserve energy and essential components of membrane lipids in all living organisms. In plants, FA metabolic pathways play significant roles in pathogen defense. Historically, FAs were only assigned passive roles in plant defense such as biosynthetic precursors for cuticular components or the phytohormone jasmonic acid. We identified three Fatty acid genes-related gene families by pfam (CRAL-TRIO lipid binding domain, Fatty acid hydroxylase and Fatty acyl-coenzyme A reductase, NAD-binding domain ), for a total of 1,997 genes.
