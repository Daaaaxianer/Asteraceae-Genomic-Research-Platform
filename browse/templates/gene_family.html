  Using the model plant Arabidopsis thaliana as a reference, 1,097,511 genes from 180 trait-associated gene families across 53 Asteraceae species and 6 outgroups have been identified. Users can determine the gene family to which a sequence belongs by performing a BLAST match. Alternatively, by selecting a gene family of interest—such as MADS-box or HSF—the webpage will display all members of that gene family across all Asteraceae plants. We developed the Fami-Tree and Fami-Circos tools for studying the phylogeny and gene duplication-driven expansion of families.
