Welcome to AGRP!

Asteraceae is the largest angiosperm family, with more than 1,700 genera and 25,000 species. Asteraceae have a strong adaptability to the environment and are widely distributed in almost all parts of the world. It contains a large number of edible, medicinal and ornamental plants, which are of great value to human life.

AGRP integrates 164 genomes, including 157 published genomes from the Asteraceae family, the newly sequenced Solidago canadensis genome, and 6 outgroup species. The platform provides annotation information for 183,430,746 transposable elements (TEs) across Asteraceae genomes, annotation details for 18,050,760 protein-coding genes along with their duplication types, resources for synteny analysis, ancestral genome resources, multi-omics resources, specialized floral fragrance metabolite resources, and an encyclopedic database of Asteraceae plants. Interfaces like gene family include tools such as Ortho-Tree, Fami-Tree, and Fami-Circos to support gene evolutionary analysis. It integrates 62 window-based operational tools (34 of which are self-developed) and 70 downloadable tools. Beyond providing intuitive query, analysis, and visualization tools, it features a data submission portal, statistical charts, and user manuals to ensure accessibility for all users. AGRP also established AGRP-Switch to support one-stop searching across all AGRP resources.
