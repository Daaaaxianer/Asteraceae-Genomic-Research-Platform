chromosome number: 2n=2X=40

Introduction: Glycine soja, known as wild soybean, is an annual plant in the legume family. It may be treated as a separate species, the closest living relative of the cultivated soybean, Glycine max, an important crop, or as a subspecies of the cultivated soybean, Glycine max subsp. soja. The plant is native to eastern China, Japan, Korea and far-eastern Russia.

Distribution: The seeds of two types of beans can be used as medicine for gynecological diseases. Both above- and below-ground parts of the bean bear seeds, which contain isoflavones with anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, antitumor and antibacterial effects.
