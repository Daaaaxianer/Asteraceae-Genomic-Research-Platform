chromosome number: 2n=2X=22

Introduction: Alfalfa (scientific name: Medicago ruthenica (L.) Trautv.), is a legume. It is a perennial herb with a well-developed root system and a main root that penetrates deeply into the soil. The inflorescence is umbellate, the involucral pedicels axillary, the bracts prickly-hairy, and the corolla yellow-brown with a central crimson to purple stripe. The pods are oblong or ovate-oblong, flattened. Its high forage value, rich in nutrients, is a variety of livestock and fish like to eat forage. In addition, alfalfa has the effect of clearing heat and detoxifying, stopping cough and bleeding, so it also has high medicinal value.

Distribution: Amur, Buryatiya, China North-Central, China South-Central, China Southeast, Chita, Inner Mongolia, Irkutsk, Khabarovsk, Korea, Manchuria, Mongolia, Primorye, Qinghai
