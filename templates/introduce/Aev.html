Overview

Specie: Aeschynomene evenia

CommonName: Shrubby jointvetch

Taxonomy: Fabaceae; Papilionoideae; 50 kb inversion clade; dalbergioids sensu lato; Dalbergieae; Dalbergia clade; Aeschynomene

Abbr: Aev

Level: Genome

chromosome number: 2n=2X=20

GenomeSize: 400 Mb

Introduction: Herbs or subshrubby annual, stems erect, 0.3 to 1 m tall. Much branched, cylindrical, glabrous, slightly scabrous with tubercles, branchlets green. Leaves with 20 to 30 pairs of leaflets or more; Stipules membranous, ovate to lanceolate, ca. 1 cm long, auricular decurrent at base, often notched or gnawed; Petiole ca. 3 mm long; Leaflets subsessile, thin papery, linear-oblong, 5 -- 10 (-15) x 2 -- 2.5 (-3.5) mm, densely glandular above, slightly whitened below, apex obtuse or slightly concave, spinely apical, base oblique, entire; Stipules are very small.

Distribution: Biogeografic region: Andean, Caribbean, Orinoquia, Pacific. Elevation range: 0–1050 m a.s.l. Native to Colombia. Colombian departments: Arauca, Atlántico, Casanare, La Guajira, Meta, Nariño, Risaralda, Valle del Cauca.

Article Title: Genetics of nodulation in Aeschynomene evenia uncovers mechanisms of the rhizobium–legume symbiosis

Data source: Genome assembly and annotation, accession resequencing and RNA-seq data for A. evenia are deposited at NCBI under BioProject ID PRJNA448804). RNA-seq data for other Aeschynomene species are available under the BioProject ID PRJNA459484. Resequencing data for A. evenia nodulation mutants are available under the BioProject ID PRJNA590707 and PRJNA590847. Accession numbers for all deposited data are given in Supplementary Data 6. Genome assembly and annotation for A. evenia can also be accessed at AeschynomeneBase (http://aeschynomenebase.fr) and the Legume Information System (https://legumeinfo.org).
