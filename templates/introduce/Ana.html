Overview

Species name: Ammopiptanthus nanus

Taxonomy: Fabaceae; Papilionoideae; 50 kb inversion clade; genistoids sensu lato; core genistoids; Sophoreae; Ammopiptanthus

Abbr: Ana

Level: Scaffold

chromosome number: 2n=2X=20

GenomeSize: 890Mb

Introduction: Evergreen shrub of ilex SPP. The crown is nearly rounded, the bark is yellow, and the stems and leaves are dense. Leaves simple, stipules very fine, conical; Petiole robust, leaf blade entire, broadly elliptic to ovate, apex obtuse, base broadly cuneate or obtuse, racemes short, terminal branch ends, flowers set; Pedicels slightly longer than calyx, bracts caducous, calyx campanulate, calyx teeth triangular, pod linear, fruit-necked, seeds larger. Distributed in Kashgar, western Xinjiang, China. It is also found in the western Tianshan Mountains. Grows on gravelly hillsides. Small sand Holly has xerophyte, cold resistance, soil barren resistance, love light.

Native range: Distributed in Inner Mongolia, Ningxia, Gansu, China. Born in dune, river beach platform, for good sand fixing plants. It is also found in southern Mongolia. Type specimens were collected from Alxa, Inner Mongolia.
