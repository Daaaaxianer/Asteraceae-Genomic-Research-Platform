Overview

Specie: Arachis duranensis

Taxonomy: Fabaceae; Papilionoideae; 50 kb inversion clade; dalbergioids sensu lato; Dalbergieae; Pterocarpus clade; Arachis

Abbr: Adu

Level: Chromosome

chromosome number: 2n=2X=20

Introduction: Arachis duranensis is also known as Arachis duranensis, false ground bean. It is a perennial evergreen persistent herb of the genus Arachis in the family Pteridophyta. Native to subtropical areas of South America, is a good ground cover plants emerging in recent years, but also good forage and green manure plants. China is more cultivated in Guangdong, Hainan, Taiwan and other places, other tropical and subtropical provinces are also promoting planting. It can be rooted at nodes, with flowers between nodes, and the grass layer is flat and uniform. The yellow flowers stick out of the lawn surface to form a full golden yellow, a carpet type cover, in the landscape configuration of the application of the landscape effect is good. Golden flower like tropical humid climate, adaptable.

Distribution: Native to the subtropical regions of South America. It is cultivated more in Guangdong, Hainan and Taiwan in China, and is also being promoted in other tropical and subtropical provinces.
