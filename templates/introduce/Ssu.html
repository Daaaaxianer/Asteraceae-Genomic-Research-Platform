Introduction: Spatholobus suberectus Dunn (scientific name: Spatholobus suberectus Dunn) is a plant of the genus Spatholobus. Climbing vine, shrubby when young. Leaflets papery or subleathery, profiled, terminal bilaterally symmetrical, broadly elliptic, broadly obovate to suborbicular, 9 -- 19 x 5 -- 14 cm, apex abruptly reduced to short caudate, cusp obtuse, base broadly cuneate. Panicles axillary or at apex of branchlets, up to 50 cm long, rachis, pedicels yellowish-brown pubescent, bracts and bracteoles linear, persistent; Calyx short, 3.5 -- 4 mm long, calyx teeth 2 -- 3 times shorter than calyx tube, lower 3 teeth apex rounded or slightly obtuse, less than 1 mm long, upper 2 teeth slightly longer. Pods subfalciform, 8-11 cm long, densely covered with short brown tomentose, base with a fruit-neck 4-9 mm long; Seeds oblong flat, about 2 cm long, about 1 cm wide, seed coat purple brown, thin and crisp, bright. Flowering in June, fruit in November - December.

Distribution: Assam, China South-Central, China Southeast, East Himalaya, Laos, Thailand, Vietnam
