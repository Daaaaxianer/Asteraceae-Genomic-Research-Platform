Overview

Specie: Arachis ipaensis

Taxonomy: Fabaceae; Papilionoideae; 50 kb inversion clade; dalbergioids sensu lato; Dalbergieae; Pterocarpus clade; Arachis

Abbr: Aip

Level: Chromosome

chromosome number: 2n=2X=20

Introduction: Arachis ipaensis is a herb in the Faboideae subfamily. This plant is cited as gene sources for research in plant biology of peanut (Arachis hypogaea). Its genome has been sequenced. The native range of this species is S. Bolivia. It grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. Annual, Not climbing, Herb.

Distribution: Bolivia

Article Title: Chromosome-length genome assemblies of six legume species provide insights into genome organization, evolution, and agronomic traits for crop improvement

Data source: The sequencing data and genome assemblies have been deposited in NCBI with the BioProject ID PRJNA679437 and PRJNA512907 (SRR14657175). The genome assemblies and annotations are available at Legumepedia (https://cegresources.icrisat.org/legumepedia/index.php). The interactive Hi-C contact maps for all the six legume genomes are available at www.dnazoo.org..
