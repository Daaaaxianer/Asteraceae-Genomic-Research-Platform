Overview

Specie: Cicer reticulatum

Taxonomy: Fabaceae; Papilionoideae; 50 kb inversion clade; NPAAA clade; Hologalegina; IRL clade; Cicereae; Cicer

Abbr: Cre

Level: *

chromosome number: 2n=2X=16

Introduction: Cicer reticulatum is a species of flowering plant in the family Fabaceae, native to southeastern Turkey. It is the wild ancestor of Cicer arietinum, the chickpea or garbanzo. It appears that Cicer reticulatum had already developed the tan-seeded kabuli and the smaller green-seeded desi types prior to the domestication of the chickpea at the dawn of agriculture.

Distribution: Iraq, Turkey

Article Title: Draft genome sequence of Cicer reticulatum L., the wild progenitor of chickpea provides a resource for agronomic trait improvement
