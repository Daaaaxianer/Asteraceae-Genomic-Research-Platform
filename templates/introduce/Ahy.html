Overview

Specie: Arachis hypogaea

Taxonomy: Fabaceae; Papilionoideae; 50 kb inversion clade; dalbergioids sensu lato; Dalbergieae; Pterocarpus clade; Arachis

Abbr: Ahy

Level: Chromosome

chromosome number: 2n=4X=40

Introduction: The native range of this species is a cultigen from Bolivia. It is an annual and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is used as animal food, a poison, a medicine and invertebrate food, has environmental uses and social uses and for fuel and food.
