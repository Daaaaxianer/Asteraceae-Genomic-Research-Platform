We collected the Latin names, associated literature, and genomic data links for 81 sequenced Asteraceae species from plaBiPD (https://www.plabipd.de). Based on the literature, we obtained Genome sequences, GFF files, nucleotide (CDS) sequences, and protein (PEP) sequences for 52 species from databases including NCBI (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/), NGDC (https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/), and figshare (https://figshare.com/).
