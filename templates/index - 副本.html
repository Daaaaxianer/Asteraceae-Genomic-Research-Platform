    Overview

    Welcome to LGRPv2

    LGRPv2(FFabaceae;3P(F:Fabaceae; 3P: Phylogenomics, Polyploidizations and Paleogenome; DB:database) is our user-friendly web-based comparative genomics platform. We summarized 14 years of research results and built 45 3P-related tools to provide practical tools for relevant researchers, which we named IPAP. In the LGRPv2 are stored 2,028 homologous structure dotplots, 27,996 and 28,164 rows in the hierarchical list of homologous gene with P. vulgaris and V. vinifera as reference, 70,673 event-related genes and genomic hybrid gene pairs, 912,552 genes, 38,690 Orthogroups, 8,470,419 gene function and 912,552 pathway map annotation information. In addition, we collected 136 genomic information resources from 58 Fabaceae and 286,467 Fabaceae related literature. We hope that the LGRPv2 will be a great resource for studying the genome and breeding of Fabaceae.
